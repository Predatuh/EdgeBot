Why this is built the way it is

Kalshi prices a combo as the product of its legs, so a fairly priced parlay wins 1 ÷ payout of the time. A 3-leg ticket paying 11× and a 39-leg ticket paying 11× are the same 9% bet — the 39-leg version just crosses 39 spreads to get there. That is why the risk dial moves the target and the builder answers with the fewest, tightest legs that reach it.

True is the de-vigged mid — every outcome in the game normalised to sum to 100%. Ask is what you actually pay. The gap between them is the whole cost of the ticket, and Edge is that gap compounded across every leg. It is always negative. A shorter ticket is a less negative one.

Draw loses. Soccer and Test cricket price three outcomes, and a draw settles a win contract at zero — so the draw stays in the denominator. A match quoted 55¢ to win between two teams is worth about 40¢ once the draw is counted, and that lower number is what you see here. Any leg that can be drawn is badged.

Prices move. Re-check each leg on Kalshi before you buy — this page is a snapshot, not a live quote.
